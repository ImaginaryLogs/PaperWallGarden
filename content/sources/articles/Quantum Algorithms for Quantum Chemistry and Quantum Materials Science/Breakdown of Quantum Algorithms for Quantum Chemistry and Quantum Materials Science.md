# 1. Introduction
The three quantum problems in chemistry and materials physics:
 1. Quantum dynamics - how quantum systems evolve over time. This is representative of computational tasks that can be efficiently tackled by a quantum computer, aside from the initial state preparation; 
 2. Quantum ground-energy and low-energy state determination, central to quantum chemistry, condensed phase electronic structure, and quantum molecular spectroscopy; and 
 3. Thermal averages or Quantum Statistical Mechanics - how many particles move in to describe many finite temperature chemistry and physics.

# 2. Simulation Challenges in Molecular and Materials Science
There are few areas to the challenges present in molecular and materials science.
1. [[Chemical Quantum Dynamics Challenges]] - the problem of determining low-lying states (ground state, multi-reference state)
2. [[Quantum Molecular Spectroscopy Challenges]] - concerns with how nuclei rotate and vibrate in molecules
3. [[Chemical Quantum Dynamics Challenges]] - the study of electronic and nuclear motion to understand molecular motion and chemical reactivity
4. [[Correlated Electronic Structure in Materials Challenges]] - a unique cousin of [[quantum chemistry]] studying unique electron interactions. This is where simulating a solid material where the electrons are so highly interactive that what one electron does instantly dictates what thousands of other neighboring electrons do.
5. [[Dynamical Quantum Effects in Materials Challenges]] -  a very difficult field of taking those highly correlated materials and trying to simulate how they _change over time_ when you hit them with an external force.
