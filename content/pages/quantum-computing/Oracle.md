In quantum computing, an **oracle** is essentially treated as a "black box" operation. It is a part of a quantum circuit that performs a specific task or evaluates a function, but the inner workings of how it does so are hidden from the algorithm using it.

In many classical algorithms, if you want to find a specific item in an unsorted database or solve a needle-in-a-haystack problem, you have to check every single item one by one.

In quantum computing, algorithms like **Grover’s Search Algorithm** or the **Deutsch-Jozsa Algorithm** use an oracle to solve these problems much faster.

The oracle's job is to "mark" the correct answer.
