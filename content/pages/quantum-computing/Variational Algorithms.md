These are are NISQ-era — designed to run on today's noisy, short-circuit hardware. The quantum computer evaluates a parameterized circuit and returns an expectation value. A classical optimizer adjusts the parameters. The quantum circuit never runs for long, so noise doesn't accumulate too badly. VQE (Variational Quantum Eigensolver) applies this to find the ground state energy of a molecule. QAOA (Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm) applies it to combinatorial optimization problems. Both are heuristic — they give approximate answers.
