FTQC represents the "holy grail" of quantum computing. It assumes the existence of **quantum error correction (QEC)**. In this paradigm, you don't use raw physical qubits; you use many physical qubits to create a single, stable **logical qubit**. Because this logical qubit is protected from environmental noise, the quantum computer can run circuits of arbitrary depth, meaning it can execute complex algorithms (like Shor’s or full-scale quantum simulation) from start to finish without needing a classical computer to "patch" it halfway through.
