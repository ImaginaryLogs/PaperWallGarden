- **Principles:** 
	- Built on **Translation Invariance** (patterns are recognized regardless of location) and 
	- **Locality** (pixels are mostly related to their neighbors).

- **Operations:** 2D Cross-correlation using **Kernels** (filters), **Stride** (step size), and **Padding**.

- **Channels:** Handling multiple input/output channels to recognize and combine complex patterns
