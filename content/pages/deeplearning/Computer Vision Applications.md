**Object Detection:** Beyond classification, this involves identifying objects via:
- **Bounding Boxes**, 
- **Anchor Boxes**, 
- **Intersection over Union (IoU)**, and 
- **Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS)**.

**Style Transfer:** Synthesizing images by matching:
- the **Content Loss** of one image and the 
- **Style Loss** (calculated via a **Gram Matrix**) of another.

**Image Classification:** Mapping pixels into predefined discrete categories, often measured by Top-1 accuracy
