- **Batch Normalization:** Fixing mean and variance per minibatch to stabilize training and act as a regularizer.
- **Data Augmentation:** Artificially expanding datasets through **Cropping**, **Color jittering**, and other transformations.
- **Transfer Learning:** Reusing a pre-trained network's **Feature Extractor** and **Fine-Tuning** it for a new target dataset.
