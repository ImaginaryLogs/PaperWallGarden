**Early Nets:** Progression from **LeNet** to **AlexNet** (which introduced ReLU and Dropout).

**Inception Blocks:** Utilizing multiple parallel paths with different convolution sizes to increase compute efficiency.

**Residual Networks (ResNet):** Introducing **Residual Connections** to allow for much deeper networks by facilitating gradient flow.
